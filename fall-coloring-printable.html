Fall Coloring Pages — 6 Cozy Autumn Designs

October sneaks up on you, doesn't it? One minute the kids are still in shorts, and the next they're crunching leaves all the way to school and asking — loudly, daily — when the pumpkins are coming. Fall is that sweet spot of the year: still warm enough to play outside, cool enough that a stack of crayons and a quiet table sounds like heaven.

These fall coloring pages are the cozy side of autumn. A plump pumpkin (the friendly kind, not the Halloween kind), three fluttery fall leaves, an acorn, a scarecrow with a stitched smile, a big sunflower, and a shiny apple. Thick outlines, big shapes — exactly what small hands need, whether they're two and gloriously scribbling or seven and taking it very seriously.

Print the whole page and cut along the dashed lines to make six little coloring cards, or keep it as one sheet for the fridge gallery. They also make the sweetest Thanksgiving table placemats — color one side now, flip it over when the turkey arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these fall coloring pages really free?

Yes — every design is free for personal and classroom use. Print one for the fridge or thirty for the whole class. No sign-up, no watermark, no catch.

How do I print the fall coloring pages?

Hit the Print button above. They come out best on plain white paper at 100% scale — one page of black-and-white line art, so any printer will do the job.

What ages are they for?

The outlines are thick and the shapes are big, which suits ages 2 to 7 best. Older kids like them too, especially as quick autumn decorations to color and cut out.

Can I use them in my classroom or homeschool co-op?

Yes — print a stack for the October art corner, a rainy-day activity, or the Thanksgiving-week wind-down when everyone's attention span has officially left the building.

What can kids do with the colored pages?

Plenty. Fridge gallery, cut-apart cards to hand out, Thanksgiving table placemats (color one side, flip it over for the snack side), or tape them in a row across the mantel as a paper garland.
