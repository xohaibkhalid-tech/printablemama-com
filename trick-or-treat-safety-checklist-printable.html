Trick-or-Treat Safety Checklist — 14 Checks for a Safe Halloween Night

Here is the thing about Halloween night: your kids do not want a safety lecture, and you do not want to be the parent giving one in the driveway while the neighbours' kids sprint past in full costume. So skip the lecture and hand them a checklist instead.

This free trick-or-treat safety checklist covers the stuff that actually matters — a costume they can walk in, something bright so drivers can see them, sticking with the group, only visiting lit-up houses, and the candy check when they get home. Fourteen boxes, one page, and the kids get to tick them off themselves, which is half the fun.

Print one per kid, run through it while the costumes go on, and then everybody heads out feeling like a team instead of a supervised outing. Safer night, happier kids, and you get to relax a little too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the trick-or-treat safety checklist really free?

Yes — it is free for personal and classroom use. Print one per kid. No sign-up, no watermark, no catch.

What ages is it for?

Ages 3 to 12. Little ones go through it with a grown-up; older kids can tick it off themselves before they head out.

Can teachers use it in the classroom?

Yes — it works as a Halloween-week safety lesson. Go through the checks together, then send a copy home in backpacks.

Does it print on one page?

Yes — the whole checklist is one page. Any printer, plain paper, done.
