Christmas Coloring Pages — 6 Jolly Holiday Designs

December with kids is a special kind of chaos, isn't it? The advent calendar chocolate is somehow gone by the 3rd, someone's already asking what's in the big box under the tree, and the phrase "is it Christmas yet?" has been uttered approximately four thousand times before breakfast.

Here's the thing nobody warns you about: the run-up to Christmas is LONG for little kids. Weeks of waiting. That's where coloring pages earn their keep — not as some Pinterest-perfect activity, but as fifteen blessed minutes of quiet while you wrap presents in the next room.

These Christmas coloring pages are the greatest hits: a Christmas tree with ornaments and a star, Santa himself (beard and all), a reindeer with proper antlers, a snowman in his top hat, a stocking with a candy cane poking out, and a gingerbread man with icing squiggles. Thick outlines, big shapes — built for small hands and big enthusiasm.

Print the whole sheet and cut along the dashed lines for six little cards, or keep it whole for the fridge. Fair warning: the gingerbread man gets colored first, every single time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these Christmas coloring pages really free?

Yes — every design is free for personal and classroom use. Print one for the fridge or thirty for the whole class. No sign-up, no watermark, no catch.

How do I print the Christmas coloring pages?

Hit the Print button above. Plain white paper, 100% scale, black-and-white line art — any printer handles it.

What ages are they for?

Thick outlines and big shapes suit ages 2 to 7 best. Older kids enjoy them as quick holiday decorations too.

Can I use them in my classroom?

Absolutely — the December art corner, the last-week-of-term wind-down, or a calm activity between the nativity rehearsal and the party.

What can kids do with the finished pages?

Cut them into gift tags (punch a hole, add some ribbon), glue them onto folded card for handmade Christmas cards, or string them into a garland across the mantel. The tree and snowman make particularly good cards.
