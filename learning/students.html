Frequently Asked Questions

I am in high school — are these too childish?

No. The student printables are designed clean and grown-up: no cartoon characters, just clear layouts that work for middle school, high school and college.

What is the difference between the study planner and the exam countdown?

The study planner organizes a normal week of studying. The exam countdown is for the final stretch — cross off one box per day until exam day.

Can I fill these in on my computer?

They are designed for printing and handwriting, which most students remember better. You can also type your name in the Personalize bar before printing.
