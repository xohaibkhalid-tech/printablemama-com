Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use these for my whole class?

Yes — print as many copies as you need for your classroom. They are free for classroom use.

Which planner should I start with?

If you plan week by week, start with the Weekly Lesson Planner. If you think day by day, the Lesson Plan template is the classic.

How do the award certificates print?

Two per page on US Letter. Type the student name in the Personalize bar and it prints right on the certificate — or write it by hand.
